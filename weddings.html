Weddings

Looking for something different for your wedding catering? At That’s Mature, we bring a relaxed, street food-style experience to your big day — the kind of food guests genuinely get excited about. From golden grilled toasties to loaded fries and late-night comfort food, we serve fresh, informal catering that keeps people coming back for “just one more.”

Perfect for evening receptions, festival-style weddings, and couples wanting something less traditional, our setup creates a laid-back atmosphere where guests can grab incredible food, mingle, and enjoy the celebration without the formality of standard wedding catering.

Whether it’s feeding guests after the dancefloor opens, serving relaxed outdoor receptions, or giving your wedding a unique talking point, That’s Mature delivers unforgettable food with personality.
